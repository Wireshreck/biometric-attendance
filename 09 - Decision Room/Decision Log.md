## Change procedure

For a material change, add a new ADR row with context, decision, consequences, evidence, and superseded ID. Update the relevant canonical plan, requirements traceability, affected task, and Canvas edge in the same milestone.

**Status vocabulary used above:** DECIDED · PROVISIONAL · PLANNED · IMPLEMENTED · VERIFIED · UNVERIFIED · DEFERRED · SUPERSEDED. `IMPLEMENTED` and `VERIFIED` are different claims and are never used interchangeably. See [[08 - Documentation Library/Documentation Library]] for the colour legend.
